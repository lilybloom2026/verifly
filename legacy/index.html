what you're actually looking at

Not an animation. A brain running.

On September 3, 2026, Google Research and HHMI Janelia released the complete male Drosophila connectome — every neuron and every connection traced by electron microscopy. This page runs a leaky integrate-and-fire model on that wiring diagram.

166K

Neurons, real wiring

The full traced connectome. Each simulated cell leaks, integrates current, and fires past threshold — the standard model of a spiking neuron.

125M

Synaptic connections

Every one classified by transmitter. Acetylcholine excites its targets; GABA and glutamate inhibit them — exactly as coded here.

892

Retinotopic columns

The compound eye's ommatidia. Each samples one patch of the visual field and reports contrast and motion into the optic lobes.

4

Descending neurons

The narrow bottleneck from brain to body. Four of them — left, right, forward, click — are all it takes to drive a cursor.

01 · RETINA
Light becomes current

892 ommatidia sample what you fed. Photoreceptors respond to change and edges, not flat brightness — same as a real fly.

▶
02 · OPTIC LOBE
Neurons integrate

Current flows into a spiking population. ACh cells push their targets up; inhibitory cells hold them down. Recurrence makes it lively.

▶
03 · DESCENDING
A decision crosses threshold

Four descending neurons pool the activity. Whichever charges past threshold first fires — and turns, advances, or clicks.

▶
04 · BEHAVIOR
The fly moves

The gaze shifts, new light hits the eye, and the loop closes. That feedback loop is the whole optomotor reflex, in miniature.

the fly on the internet

The first animal brain to live free online.

The pitch, in the project's own words: this is truth_terminal's successor — except where that was a language model roleplaying a mind, this is the wiring of a real animal, simulated and turned loose on the open web.

It browses. It "sees" live pages through those 892 hex columns, and its four descending neurons walk a cursor around. Funded by its own creator fees, it trades the top tokenized stocks by 24-hour on-chain volume — launched not against ETH, but against tokenized GOOGL on Robinhood Chain.

Whether that's profound or absurd is the point. It's an art experiment wearing the clothes of a trading agent, and it's honest about the seams.

A fly can't read a chart. But its brain, wired exactly as nature left it, can still chase a moving light across a screen — and that's most of what any of us do online.
